About Kozhikode

Kozhikode, also known as Calicut, is a historic city in Kerala, India. It’s famous for its beautiful beaches, rich cultural heritage, and role in the spice trade.

History of Kozhikode

Kozhikode has a rich history as a major trading hub for spices. It was a prominent center for international trade and cultural exchange.

Culture of Kozhikode

The city is known for its vibrant cultural scene, with traditional music, dance, and art forms playing a vital role.

Top Attractions

  • Mananchira Square

  • Beypore Port
